Piglet Mortality Reduction Vision System

AI-powered monitoring for early detection of piglet distress

Computer Vision Python Machine Learning IoT

Project Overview

The Piglet Mortality Reduction Vision System uses computer vision and machine learning to monitor piglet behavior in farrowing pens. By detecting early signs of distress or abnormal behavior, the system alerts farmers to potential issues before they become critical, significantly reducing mortality rates.

System Tracking Demo

Real-time tracking demonstration showing the computer vision system identifying and monitoring individual piglets in the farrowing pen. The system successfully maintains tracking even with overlapping subjects and varying lighting conditions.

Key Features

  • Real-time video analysis of piglet behavior
  • Machine learning model for behavior classification
  • Automated detection of crushing events and distress signals
  • Temperature and environmental monitoring
  • Mobile alerts for immediate farmer notification
  • Historical data tracking and analytics dashboard

Technical Details

Computer Vision

  • OpenCV for image processing
  • YOLO for object detection
  • Optical flow for movement tracking
  • Thermal imaging integration
  • Multi-camera synchronization

Machine Learning

  • TensorFlow/PyTorch frameworks
  • Custom CNN for behavior classification
  • Transfer learning from pre-trained models
  • Real-time inference optimization
  • Continuous model improvement pipeline

Challenges & Solutions

Occlusion and Overlapping

Situation: Piglets in farrowing pens frequently overlap and occlude each other, creating complex visual scenarios.

Task: Develop a tracking system that maintains individual piglet identity even when they are partially or fully hidden by other piglets.

Action: Implemented a multi-camera system with overhead and side views, combined with advanced tracking algorithms that use motion prediction and feature matching to maintain identity across frames.

Result: Achieved 92% tracking accuracy even during high-occlusion events, enabling reliable behavioral monitoring throughout the day.

Low-Light Conditions

Situation: Farrowing pens often have limited lighting during night hours to avoid disturbing the animals' natural sleep cycles.

Task: Enable 24/7 monitoring without introducing artificial lighting that could stress the animals or disrupt their behavior.

Action: Integrated infrared cameras and thermal imaging sensors that operate effectively in complete darkness, paired with computer vision algorithms optimized for low-light image processing.

Result: Maintained consistent detection accuracy day and night, reducing mortality rates by enabling round-the-clock distress detection.

Results & Impact

Early testing showed the system can detect distress events with 92% accuracy and reduce response time from hours to minutes. Preliminary field trials demonstrated a potential 25% reduction in pre-weaning mortality rates, which could save significant livestock and improve animal welfare.
