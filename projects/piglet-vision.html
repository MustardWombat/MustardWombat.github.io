Piglet Keypoint Detection System

MMPOSE-based model for anatomical keypoint detection in piglets

Computer Vision Python MMPOSE Deep Learning

Project Overview

The Piglet Keypoint Detection System utilizes MMPOSE, a state-of-the-art pose estimation framework, to detect anatomical keypoints on piglets in images and video. The model identifies seven keypoints: snout, head, left ear, right ear, shoulder, back, and tail. This technical solution enables robust keypoint localization across diverse lighting conditions and piglet poses.

Custom datasets were curated and annotated to ensure accurate keypoint detection. The system is designed for high performance and cross-platform compatibility, with ONNX model export and configurable camera stream input.

Challenges & Solutions

Occlusion & Keypoint Visibility

Challenge: Piglets in group settings often overlap, causing partial occlusions and making some keypoints difficult to annotate or detect.

Solution: Applied targeted data augmentation strategies to simulate occlusion scenarios and improve model robustness. Used MMPOSE's advanced feature extraction to enhance keypoint localization even under partial visibility.

Lighting & Pose Diversity

Challenge: Diverse lighting conditions and piglet poses introduce variability in image appearance and keypoint locations.

Solution: Curated dataset with images from multiple lighting environments and piglet poses. Used augmentation (rotation, brightness, flip) to improve generalization.
